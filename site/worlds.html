Worlds library

The Worlds page lists your local worlds with their icon, the last-saved date and the size. You can refresh, search and sort.

Your files stay put#

  • The installer and uninstaller leave worlds and resource packs on disk.
  • The launcher does not upload your worlds anywhere.

Backups#

One-click backup and restore for worlds is on the roadmap. It is not built yet.

Warning: Back up important worlds before you change game files or restore anything. Copy the world folder somewhere safe.

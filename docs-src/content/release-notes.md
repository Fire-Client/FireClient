## FireClient 1.1

Announced on 20 October 2026 on the [news page](https://www.fireclient.net/news/fireclient-1-1-device-scaling-crash-fix-update/):

- Adaptive screen scaling, from compact laptops to 4K monitors
- A built-in Pro Crash Reporter with one-click auto-fix
- A Potato PC booster for low-end machines
- A published license and safety facts page

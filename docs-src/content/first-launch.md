## 1. Choose how to play

You do not have to sign in. Pick **Offline Mode** to play singleplayer or on offline-mode servers with no account. Pick **Online Mode** to sign in with Microsoft. Details are on the [accounts page](accounts.md).

## 2. Pick a version

Open the **Versions** page and choose Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric. The launcher installs the game and the Java runtime for you. A live console shows progress and any errors.

## 3. Launch

Press **Launch** on the Home page. When the game opens you will see the FireClient title screen.

## 4. Open the menu

Press <kbd>Right Shift</kbd> in a world, or use the **FireClient Menu** button on the title screen. See [Set up the HUD](hud.md) for the rest.

> **Tip:** The in-game menu only exists for Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric. Other versions start fine but do not have it. See [Versions, profiles and Java](versions-profiles.md).

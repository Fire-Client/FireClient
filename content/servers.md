## Saved servers

The Servers page keeps the servers you play on. Star up to 10 and they appear on Home. Online-mode servers need an [online account](accounts.md).

## Host a Paper server

The Host Server page runs and manages a local PaperMC server.

1. Open **Host Server**.
2. Start the server.
3. Wait for the status to show **Running**. That means Paper reports it is ready.

## Port forwarding

The launcher does not set up your router. For friends outside your network to join, forward the port yourself.

> **Tip:** Test with a friend on your own network first. If that works and outside players cannot join, the problem is your router.

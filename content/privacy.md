FireClient only talks to a service when you use the feature that needs it.

| Feature | Where it connects |
|---|---|
| Signing in | Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft services |
| Downloads and update checks | The provider of what you get: Mojang and Microsoft, Adoptium, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Modrinth, PaperMC, GitHub and their CDNs |
| Mod library scan | Sends file hashes to Modrinth to recognise your mods |
| Community features | Connect to justiceclient.org |
| Discord presence | Your local Discord app only |

Downloads see your IP address and normal request information, like any download.

## Community features

Profile, friends, messages, server listings and a marketplace are optional. Nothing is sent unless you use them.

## Logs

Logs and crash logs stay on your machine. The launcher never uploads them. A copy-diagnostics button helps when you ask for support, and you choose where to paste the result.
